Four questions, asked of anything you're sure about.

Confidence in your readAlmost certain · untested
ImpossibleCertain
  1. One of four

    What else could explain this?

    Not weaker versions of your view. The strongest rivals to it.

  2. Two of four

    What would we expect to see if that were true?

    An explanation that predicts nothing can't be tested. Each one has to say what it would look like.

  3. Three of four

    Which observation would tell them apart?

    Most of what you've noticed fits every explanation. A little of it doesn't. That little is the evidence.

  4. Four of four

    Do you know that?

    Often the fact that settles it is one you already have and hadn't used. Sometimes it is one question away.
